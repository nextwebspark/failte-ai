---
title: "Call summary emails"
description: "Email the business after each phone call where the agent took a message or booked or cancelled an appointment."
---

An agent can email a short summary after each phone call that leaves the team something to act on: the caller left a callback number, or the agent booked or cancelled an appointment in a connected [Google Calendar](/voice-agent/tools/integrations). Calls where the agent only answered a question send nothing.

The **AI Receptionist** asks where to send them while you set it up (**Send call summaries to**, filled in with your own address). Leave it empty to turn summaries off.

## Change who gets them

Open the agent, go to **Advanced settings → Call summaries**, and turn on **Email call summaries**. Enter up to five addresses, separated by commas, then save and publish the agent.

## What the email contains

- The agent's name and when the call started, in your workspace's time zone (**Settings → Preferences**; UTC when none is set)
- The caller's name, callback number and reason, when the agent captured them (the receptionist captures `caller_name`, `callback_number` and `call_reason`)
- Each appointment booked or cancelled during the call
- A link to the call in LightMove.ai

The email never links to the recording or transcript. They stay behind sign-in on the call page.

## When it is sent

- Phone calls only. Web calls from the editor and from the website widget, and text chats, send no summary.
- Once per call, after the call ends and its details are saved. A summary is never sent twice, even if post-call processing runs again.
- A failed send is retried twice, a few seconds apart. It never affects the call itself.

<Note>
Self-hosting? Emails need an email provider (`EMAIL_PROVIDER=smtp` or `resend`). With `EMAIL_PROVIDER=none` summaries are skipped and their content is not written to the log.
</Note>

Need the call data in another system instead? Use a [Webhook](/voice-agent/webhook) node.
