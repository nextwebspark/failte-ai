---
title: "Export or Delete Your Data"
description: "Download your account or organization data, delete your account, and delete an organization with its 30-day grace period"
---

You can take a copy of your data and delete it yourself, without contacting support. Everything on this page is in the app under **Your account** (in the menu behind your initials, open to every member) and, for admins, **Workspace settings → Danger zone**.

## Export your data

| Export | Who | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Export my data** | Every member | Your profile, the organizations you belong to and your role in each, and your onboarding progress |
| **Export organization data** | Admins | Organization settings, members and roles, agents with their current and published versions, tools, campaigns (names and progress, not contact lists), the knowledge base file list, prompt recording transcripts, phone numbers, widget and API key metadata, and the billing account and ledger |

Both download as a JSON file. Exports never contain secrets: provider API keys, telephony credentials, tool credentials, API keys and passwords are left out. The organization export also leaves out call content (recordings, transcripts and caller details); those follow your [call data retention period](/developer/environment-variables#data-retention).

## Delete your account

Open **Your account**, choose **Delete my account** and type your email address to confirm. Deleting your account:

- signs you out everywhere and deletes your login, settings and memberships;
- archives the API keys you created;
- keeps everything you built (agents, tools, campaigns, knowledge base files, widgets) in your organizations, so your colleagues' agents keep working.

You cannot delete your account while you are:

- **the only admin of an organization that has other members**: make another member an admin on the **Team** page first;
- **the only member of an organization**: delete that organization first. If you are its admin and it is the organization you have selected, the dialog offers to do that for you.

## Delete an organization

Admins can delete the selected organization from **Workspace settings → Danger zone → Delete organization** by typing its name.

The organization closes as soon as you confirm:

- its API, apart from the export and the cancel, stops answering (requests get `423` with the code `organization_pending_deletion`);
- its API keys, website widgets and API triggers are switched off, running and syncing campaigns are paused, and calls to its phone numbers are refused;
- every member sees a banner with the date the organization will be erased.

<Note>
You can cancel until that date: use **Cancel deletion** on the banner or in the Danger zone. Cancelling switches back on exactly what the deletion switched off; anything that was already off stays off. Paused campaigns resume where they stopped.
</Note>

30 days after the request, a daily job permanently erases what the organization keeps in LightMove.ai: call recordings and transcripts, agents and their versions, campaigns and contact lists, knowledge base files, prompt recordings, tools, credentials, phone numbers, settings, and its integration connections (their OAuth grants are revoked with Google where possible, and imported website catalogues are deleted). Billing accounts, the credit ledger and usage records are kept, because invoices and tax records must be retained. Members keep their own accounts and can delete them separately; members of other organizations are moved to one of those.

### What deletion does not reach

Erasure covers the data LightMove.ai stores. It cannot recall copies that have already left it:

- **Webhooks, call events and integrations.** Call data your agents sent to your own endpoints, a call-events destination, or a connected app (a CRM, a spreadsheet, a calendar) stays there. Delete it in those systems.
- **Your telephony provider.** Your carrier (Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx and so on) keeps its own call records under its own retention. Delete them in the provider's console if you need to.
- **Model providers.** Speech, language and voice providers you configured with your own keys process call audio and text under their terms with you.
- **Langfuse traces.** Traces sent to your own Langfuse project are yours to delete there.
- **Database backups.** Erased data can survive in backups until they expire.

If you self-host, the same applies to anything you added: product analytics such as PostHog (when `POSTHOG_API_KEY` is set it receives user and organization identifiers and events; delete those people and the organization group in PostHog), a platform-wide Langfuse project (delete the organization's traces there), and your log storage and backups (they expire on their own retention; shorten it or remove the data if a request requires it).

## Self-hosting

The erasure job runs every day at 03:47 UTC. Set `ORG_ERASURE_ENABLED=false` to pause it; deletion requests are still accepted and the organizations stay closed until the job runs again. See [Environment variables](/developer/environment-variables#organization-deletion).
