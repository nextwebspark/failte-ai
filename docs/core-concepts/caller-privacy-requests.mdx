---
title: "Handling caller privacy requests"
description: "Give a caller a copy of their call data, or erase it, when they ask under GDPR"
---

The people your agents talk to can ask you for a copy of the data you hold about them (GDPR Article 15) or for it to be deleted (Article 17). For call data you are the controller and LightMove.ai processes it on your behalf, so you answer these requests, and LightMove.ai gives you the tools to do it for the data it stores.

<Note>
Only organization **Admins** can export or erase call data. Developers and Viewers don't see these options.
</Note>

## What is erased and what is kept

Erasing a call deletes this personal data from LightMove.ai:

- the recording, including the separate caller and agent tracks
- the transcript and the live transcript events
- the call context: phone numbers, template variables, extracted variables and other gathered data
- QA results and the public share link
- the payloads of webhooks sent for the call, and the browser details (IP address, user agent) of a website widget call
- for a text chat, the conversation

The run itself stays in your call history with its **date, duration, status, disposition and cost**. Your billing records refer to it and have to be kept, so the numbers you are invoiced for still add up. None of these kept fields contains the caller's number or what was said.

Copies that have already left LightMove.ai are not erased. See [Things to do outside LightMove.ai](#things-to-do-outside-lightmove-ai).

## Delete one call

1. Open the run from **Agent runs** or the agent's runs list.
2. Click **Delete call data** and confirm.

A call can't be deleted while it is in progress, or in the few minutes after it ends while LightMove.ai finishes processing it (QA, webhooks, uploading the recording). Try again shortly.

## Answer a caller's request

A caller usually identifies themselves by their phone number. Use it to find every call with them across all your agents.

1. Go to **Agent runs** and click **Caller data request**.
2. Enter the caller's phone number in international format, for example `+353 87 123 4567`. If all your phone numbers are in one country, you can also use national format, for example `087 123 4567`.
3. Choose what to do:
   - **Download data (JSON)** saves a file with every call with that number: date, agent, direction, duration, status, the call context and gathered data, the transcript, and links to the recordings. It also lists the number's campaign contacts, including ones not called yet. Recording links in the file expire after one hour, so download the recordings you need straight away.
   - **Erase all data** erases every one of those calls as described above. Campaign contacts not yet called are removed so the number is never called, contact rows of calls already made are cleared, and the number's rows are removed from uploaded campaign CSV files. A summary shows what was erased.

A call matches when the number is the **other party** of the call: the caller of an inbound call, or the number called on an outbound or campaign call. Numbers are compared in any format your telephony provider stored them in: `+353 87 123 4567`, `00353871234567` and, for a provider that sends national numbers, `0871234567` all match. National-format numbers are read in the countries of your own phone numbers.

<Warning>
You can't search for one of your own phone numbers: it is on every call you make or receive. Enter the caller's number instead.
</Warning>

### When something is skipped

The erase summary lists anything it couldn't erase:

- **Calls still in progress**, or still being processed after they ended, are left untouched. Run the erase again in a few minutes.
- **Calls whose recordings couldn't be deleted** (for example during a storage outage) are left untouched, so nothing is half-erased. Try again shortly.
- **Campaign contacts being dialled right now** are skipped. Run the erase again once that call ends.
- **Contact lists that couldn't be rewritten** are named by campaign. Try again shortly.

Erasing is safe to repeat: running it again erases anything written to a call since the last time.

## Things to do outside LightMove.ai

LightMove.ai can only erase what it stores. Handle these copies yourself:

- **Webhook receivers and integrations.** Data your agents sent to other systems through [webhooks](/developer/webhooks), tools, MCP servers or integrations is held by those systems. Erase it there.
- **Observability tools.** If you connected [Langfuse tracing](/configurations/tracing), its traces contain prompts, transcripts and call variables. If you use [Noveum](/integrations/noveum), it holds the audio and transcripts it was sent. Delete the caller's data in those tools.
- **Your telephony provider.** Your carrier keeps its own call records, and may keep recordings if you enabled recording there.
- **Campaign sources outside LightMove.ai.** Contact lists read from an external source, such as a Google Sheet, are not changed. Remove the caller there.
- **Your own systems.** Exports, downloaded recordings and reports you saved.

## Keeping data no longer than needed

Separately from these requests, LightMove.ai deletes call recordings, transcripts and call content automatically once they are older than your organization's **Call data retention (days)** setting, in **Settings → Preferences**.

## Using the API

The same actions are available to Admin users through the API. The number goes in the request body, so it stays out of URLs and access logs.

| Action | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Delete one call's data | `DELETE /api/v1/workflow/{workflow_id}/runs/{run_id}` |
| Export a caller's data | `POST /api/v1/organizations/usage/subject-data/export` with `{"number": "..."}` |
| Erase a caller's data | `POST /api/v1/organizations/usage/subject-data/erase` with `{"number": "..."}` |
